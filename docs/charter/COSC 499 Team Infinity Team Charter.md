### **2\. Mission Statement / Project Goal**

Project: TA Portal

Goal: To create a responsive web application that supports managing the CMPS department to allocate and manage TA allocation to courses and feedback (not an auto-scheduling system)

* An aspiring TA can create a profile, upload their CV to apply for positions, list exams so there will be no conflict when marking other exams, easily reapply for new positions, and get notified when positions are available  
* Instructors and the TA-coordinator/staff can visualize who has been assigned to each course  
* Instructors and the TA-coordinator/staff can download and visualize all allocations  
* Instructors can upload TA data from previous years and course schedule for current year  
* The TA-coordinator/staff can view up-to-date information on instructor needs and use it for their job of matching courses with TAs.

Why does it matter?

* Facilitating the TA application process for instructors, TAs, and TA-coordinators.  
* For the TA, it allows TA applicants to more easily reapply and know when applications are out.  
* For the TA-coordinator, it allows them to read the data the instructors uploaded and facilitates their job of matching courses with TAs.  
* For the instructor, it allows them to upload the data of their course so that the TA-coordinators and the TA can view them.
